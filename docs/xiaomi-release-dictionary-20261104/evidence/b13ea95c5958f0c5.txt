Space: Redefining What an SUV Interior Can Do Xiaomi's approach starts from a simple question: why should people adapt to the space, instead of the space adapting to them? Traditional large SUVs fix styling, powertrain, and chassis first, and space is whatever is left over — fixed seat orientation, uneven fold-flat surfaces, not enough room for everyone's luggage. Xiaomi SkyNomad's Kunlun Platform reverses that: it defines what the space needs to do first, then engineers the architecture around it. Xiaomi Kunlun Platform — built specifically for space. Xiaomi Kunlun Platform optimizes packaging across three axes to minimize structural intrusion into the cabin: X-axis — Front cabin integration frees interior space: A five-in-one EREV system, integrated water-cooling module, integrated refrigerant module, mid-mounted HVAC unit, and PTC-compressor two-in-one compress the front cabin length to just 1,265 mm. Every millimeter saved up front translates directly into more usable space inside. Y-axis — Third-row wheel-arch optimization: Springs and shock absorbers are separated into a split layout, releasing 15 mm of wheel-arch height for the third row compared to traditional large SUVs. Moving the shock absorbers outward with a shallow-angle installation optimizes the overall wheel-arch packaging, combined with third-row HVAC ducting redesign, delivering a squared-off, full-cabin spatial experience. Z-axis — Eliminating the step between the second and third rows: Focused on rear electric drive unit integration and rear suspension design, a highly integrated rear coaxial electric drive frees 145 mm of vertical space. An integrated-linkage independent suspension uses an H-arm structure where the air spring sits inside the arm, lowering the floor by another 37 mm. Additionally, the large battery pack is redesigned with the battery pack control module relocated inside, reducing floor thickness by 180 mm — achieving a completely flat second-row floor. The result is a flat floor running from row one through the cargo area — uninterrupted, and unusual for the large extended-range SUV segment. Every structural line is built toward the same goal with Xiaomi Kunlun Platform: the largest, flattest, most flexible space for the people inside.
