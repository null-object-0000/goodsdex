Xiaomi Smartphones Maintain Top-Three Global Ranking as Premiumization Strategy Delivers Strong Results The Group’s smartphone business made steady progress in 2025, with revenue reaching RMB186.4 billion and global shipments totaling 165.2 million units. According to Omdia, Xiaomi maintained its top 3 global smartphone shipment ranking for the 5th consecutive year, with a market share of 13.3%. In the Chinese Mainland, Xiaomi ranked No. 2 in terms of smartphone units sold, with a market share of 16.6%, up 0.7 percentage points year-over-year. Xiaomi’s premiumization strategy accelerated and delivered solid results. According to third-party data, in 2025, premium smartphones (retail price at or above RMB3,000) accounted for a record-high 27.1% of the Group’s total smartphone units sold in the Chinese Mainland. In the RMB4,000–6,000 price segment, the Group’s smartphone market share by units sold reached 17.3%. Xiaomi continued to deepen its presence in global markets. Xiaomi smartphone shipments ranked among the top three in 58 countries and regions globally and among the top five in 70 countries and regions globally. In Latin America and Southeast Asia, Xiaomi rose to No. 2, with market share reaching 17.5% and 17.0%, respectively. In Europe and Africa, Xiaomi ranked No. 3, with market share rising by 0.6 and 1.4 percentage points year-over-year to 20.3% and 12.7%, respectively. In December 2025, the MAU of the Xiaomi Home App grew by 11.7% year-over-year to 112.7 million. The Group’s internet services business maintained solid growth.
