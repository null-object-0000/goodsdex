Beijing, CHINA, May 22, 2025 Xiaomi Corporation ("Xiaomi" or the "Group"; stock code:1810), a consumer electronics and smart manufacturing company with smartphones and smart hardware connected by an Internet of Things ("IoT") platform at its core, held a product launch event under the theme "A New Beginning" in Beijing. Xiaomi officially unveiled Xiaomi XRING O1 3nm SoC, Xiaomi XRING T1 4G smartwatch SoC, Xiaomi 15S Pro, Xiaomi Pad 7 Ultra, and multiple AIoT products to its domestic market. Xiaomi Introduces Xiaomi XRING O1 3nm SoC and Xiaomi XRING T1 4G smartwatch SoC During the launch, Xiaomi unveiled its first flagship processor, Xiaomi XRING O1 3nm SoC, and Xiaomi XRING T1 4G smartwatch SoC, marking a pivotal moment in its chip development. Xiaomi XRING O1, built on a cutting-edge second-gen 3 nm process with 19 billion transistors, features a 10-core CPU and 16-core Immortalis-G925 GPU, delivering flagship performance with industry-leading power efficiency. It also integrates Xiaomi's fourth-gen ISP and a 6-core NPU offering 44 TOPS for advanced AI processing. Launching first in domestic China with Xiaomi 15S Pro and Pad 7 Ultra. Xiaomi XRING T1 is Xiaomi's first 4G smartwatch chip with a self-developed 4G baseband, marking a key milestone in its chip development journey. It supports eSIM for independent communication and delivers industry-leading battery efficiency. Backed by 7,000+ lab tests and over a year of real-world tuning across 100+ cities, the chip improves 4G performance by 35% over competitors, while reducing power consumption for voice and data. It also enables features like customizable watch faces, remote camera preview, and smartphone-free control of Xiaomi vehicles. Xiaomi XRING T1 will be introduced in the new Xiaomi Watch S4 15th Anniversary Version. These chips represent more than a technical milestone — they're a statement of Xiaomi's long-term commitment to becoming a leader in core technologies. Backed by 13.5 billion RMB in R&D to date, a planned 50 billion RMB investment over the next decade, and a team of over 2,500 engineers, Xiaomi is committing to deep, sustained innovation in chip development. Chip development is now fully integrated within Xiaomi's core foundational technologies — OS, AI, and chip, aligning hardware, software, and user experience under one vision. Marking 15 Years of Xiaomi: Xiaomi 15S Pro launched with Xiaomi XRING O1 The newly launched Xiaomi 15S Pro marks a major leap in design, performance, and intelligent functionality. It introduces two striking new finishes: the Xiaomi Composite Fiber Edition, crafted from aramid fiber with a carbon-fiber texture, and a Sky Blue variant with a matte fiberglass back offering a refined, soft metallic sheen. Building on the acclaimed Xiaomi 15 Series design, it retains the symmetrical micro-curved display, aerospace-grade aluminum frame, and a ceramic volcano-shaped camera deco, while adding premium flourishes like a gold-accented CNC power key, an "XRING" nameplate, and exclusive packaging. At the core of the device is Xiaomi XRING O1 3nm SoC, Xiaomi's first self-developed flagship SoC built on a 3 nm process, with 19 billion transistors and a 10-core CPU architecture, including dual Cortex-X925 cores at 3.9GHz. It's paired with a 16-core Immortalis-G925 GPU, achieving 3 million points on AnTuTu benchmarks. Efficiency is boosted further with a new cooling design that boosts heat dissipation by 30%, four dynamic low-power modes, and a dedicated micro-control unit. The device also boasts a 6,100 mAh battery, a Surge G1 battery management chip, and a Surge P3 charging chip. It delivers exceptional longevity even with a 2K 3200-nit LTM display using M9 materials for 18% less power draw. On the AI front, a 6-core NPU with 44 TOPS powers features like AI writing, semantic search, and notification summaries. Imaging is equally flagship-class, with a Leica Summilux triple-lens system (14mm–120mm), f/1.44 main aperture, fourth-gen ISP with real-time HDR and AI noise reduction, and stunning 4K night video performance. The 15S Pro also supports UWB digital keys, transit cards, motion sickness mitigation during mobile vehicle use, and enhanced navigation — delivering a comprehensive flagship experience engineered for power, intelligence, and elegance. Xiaomi 15S Pro is available in Xiaomi Composite Fiber and Sky Blue variants, with a starting price of 5499 RMB. Xiaomi Pad 7 Ultra is available in Misty Purple and Classic Black variants, with a starting price of 5699 RMB. About Xiaomi Corporation Xiaomi Corporation was founded in April 2010 and listed on the Main Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on July 9, 2018 ( 1810.HK ). Xiaomi is a consumer electronics and smart manufacturing company with smartphones and smart hardware connected by an IoT platform at its core. Embracing our vision of “Make friends with users and be the coolest company in the users’ hearts”, Xiaomi continuously pursues innovations, high-quality user experience and operational efficiency. The company relentlessly builds amazing products with honest prices to let everyone in the world enjoy a better life through innovative technology. Xiaomi is one of the world’s leading smartphone companies. In December 2024, the MAU reached approximately 702.3 million (including smartphones and tablets) globally. The company has also established the world’s leading consumer AIoT (AI+IoT) platform, reached approximately 904.6 million smart devices connected to its platform (excluding smartphones, laptops and tablets) as of December 31, 2024. In October 2023, Xiaomi upgraded its strategy to “Human × Car × Home” smart ecosystem, seamlessly merges personal devices, smart home products, and cars. Xiaomi always centers on humanity and is committed to providing comprehensive, better connected experiences. Xiaomi products are present in more than 100 countries and regions around the world. In August 2024, Xiaomi was included in the Fortune Global 500 list for the sixth year in a row. Xiaomi is a constituent of the Hang Seng Index, Hang Seng China Enterprises Index, Hang Seng TECH Index and Hang Seng China 50 Index.
