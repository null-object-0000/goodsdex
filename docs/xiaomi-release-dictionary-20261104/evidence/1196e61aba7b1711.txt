Beijing, China, October 29 2024 — Xiaomi held one of its most important domestic launch events of the year, officially introducing Xiaomi Smart Band 9 Pro, Xiaomi Watch S4, Xiaomi Mesh System BE3600 Pro, Xiaomi Smart Door Lock M30 Series, Xiaomi 165W Power Bank 10000 (Integrated cable), Mijia Air Conditioner Top-Outlet Pro 1.5HP Level 1+ Energy Efficiency, Mijia Multi-zone Refrigerator 4 Glass Door 508L Pro, Xiaomi TV S Pro Mini LED 2025, Mijia Dual-drum Dual Washer Dryer 10kg, and Mijia Advanced Washer Dryer 10kg Pro to Mainland China. Xiaomi Smart Band 9 Pro Xiaomi Smart Band 9 Pro pushes the limits of large-screen fitness trackers. It features a 1.74-inch AMOLED display with sleek design and high brightness, delivering exceptional visual experience. Its aluminum alloy body combines stylish design with durable materials, enhancing its premium feel. Xiaomi Smart Band 9 Pro supports comprehensive health monitoring, including heart rate, blood oxygen level, stress, and sleep analysis, developed in collaboration with three major medical institutions to improve sleep services. Users can choose from over 150 sports modes, with enhanced tracking for swimming and running to ensure accurate data, while offering an impressive battery life of up to 21 days. Xiaomi Smart Band 9 Pro starts at 399 RMB, Xiaomi Magnetic Quick Release Strap at 169 RMB, and Xiaomi Leather Quick Release Strap at 99 RMB. Xiaomi Watch S4 Xiaomi Watch S4 combines stylish design with practical features, featuring a versatile bezel and rotating crown for enhanced usability. It sports a 1.43-inch AMOLED display and supports over 150 sports modes as well as comprehensive health monitoring, including heart rate, blood oxygen level, and stress tracking. Xiaomi Watch S4 also offers built-in running training courses and AI-driven route planning. For outdoor activities, its dual-mode walkie-talkie function and eSIM support allow users to enjoy music and make calls without a phone. Users can reply to messages, send emojis, and control smart home devices directly from the watch interface. Additional features include waterproofing, customizable watch faces, sleep monitoring, NFC car key functionality, and voice assistant support, making it an excellent choice for those seeking both style and functionality. Xiaomi Watch S4 starts at 999 RMB, with Xiaomi Watch S4 Bezel and Strap Kit available in multiple colors priced from 149 RMB. Xiaomi Mesh System BE3600 Pro Xiaomi Mesh System BE3600 Pro is the ultimate smart router solution. This router set includes a main router and secondary unit, creating a robust Mesh network that covers up to 160 m² and supports up to 10 devices. Powered by a Qualcomm high-performance processor, it ensures smooth opera tion and Xiaomi HyperOS Connect , simp lifying smart home connectivity with a built-in central gateway and Bluetooth Mesh capabilities. Additionally, the design replicates unique Xiaomi router's aesthetics, featuring a hidden antenna layout that is both sleek and visually appealing, perfectly aligning with home decor and seamlessly integrating into various environments. Xiaomi Mesh System BE3600 Pro will be available from a bundle price of 699 RMB and a single unit price of 349 RMB. Xiaomi Smart Door Lock M30 Series Xiaomi Smart Door Lock M30 Series is the brand's flagship smart lock, enhancing unlocking experiences and security monitoring capabilities. With various unlocking methods, it meets diverse needs across complex scenarios. The series includes three models: First, using advanced palm vein recognition for hands-free access; second, utilizing 3D structured light for easy unlocking by simply approaching; and third, a comprehensive version that integrates palm, facial, and AI fingerprint recognition. All models suppo rt Xiaomi HyperOS Connect , enabling seamless interactions with smart devices for automated home functions, such as turning on lights when you arrive and monitoring the door area via smartphones or smart TVs. Xiaomi 165W Power Bank 10000 (Integrated Cable) Xiaomi 165W Power Bank 10000 is a newly portable and easy-to-use fast-charging power bank with built-in USB-C cable and an additional USB-C port, allowing for quick charging of mobile devices without extra cables. It offers a maximum output of 120W for a single port. It can reach up to 165W when both ports are used simultaneously, making it capable of charging smartphones and tablets and even providing emergency power to some laptops. This power bank also includes a colorful smart display to easily monitor charging and power discharge and check the current battery level. Supporting efficient discharge and fast recharging, it allows for 90W rapid self-recharging using the built-in cable, and its compact design makes it convenient to carry on airplanes. Xiaomi 165W Power Bank 10000 will be available from 199 RMB. Mijia Air Conditioner Top-Outlet Pro 1.5HP Level 1+ Energy Efficiency Mijia Air Conditioner Top-Outlet Pro is an innovative air conditioner with top-out airflow. By reconstructing the internal structure, it's equipped with a 108mm mega cross-flow fan, enabling the circulation of air volume up to 830 ㎡ /h. A series of innovative designs and configuration updates have made these air conditioners one of the industry's top choices in aesthetics and function. Mijia Air Conditioner Top-Outlet Pro enjoys an APF 5.65 Level 1+ Energy Efficiency and Xiaomi's self-developed Lingyun Intelligent Control Engine, boosting its comprehensive energy-saving rate to 40%. It also achieves smart control through multiple interactions, such as an app, voice control, and connectivity with air purifiers, humidifiers, thermometers, or other intelligent devices. Mijia Air Conditioner Top-Outlet Pro will be available from 3,499 RMB. Mijia Multi-zone Refrigerator 4 Glass Door 508L Pro Mijia Multi-zone Refrigerator 4 Glass Door 508L Pro is Xiaomi's first dual-system refrigerator, providing outstanding fresh-keeping performance that avoids unpleasant odors across compartments and extends the freshness of stored food. Mijia Multi-zone Refrigerator 4 Glass Door 508L Pro is equipped with separate sets of evaporators and fans for the refrigerator and freezer to achieve more precise temperature control. A leading 20mm ultra-thin fan and low thermal-conductivity foaming technology ensure no sacrifice of storage space due to the dual system. The water molecular sieve for the fruit and vegetable drawer and full-range purification system for refrigeration and freezing further enhance overall performance in sterilization, purifying, and preservation. Mijia Multi-zone Refrigerator 4 Glass Door 508L Pro will be available from 3,999 RMB. Xiaomi TV S Pro Mini LED 2025 A newly upgraded offering of the S Pro series, Xiaomi TV S Pro Mini LED 2025 has dramatically improved Mini LED's basic partition and brightness configuration. The 85-inch model offers partitions reaching 2,880, and the peak brightness has been increased to 3,200 nits. An additional 30W bass unit has been added to the audio system to boost bass performance. Xiaomi TV S Pro Mini LED 2025 also introduces multiple software upgrades powered by an advanced picture engine, enabling more precise picture color accuracy and more delicate transitions from light to dark. Xiaomi HyperOS 2 further enhances the overall viewing experience and smart interaction capabilities. Xiaomi TV S Pro Mini LED 2025 series is available in four sizes: 65", 75", 85", and 100", and will be available from 4,599 RMB, 6,499 RMB, 8,499 RMB, and 12,999 RMB, respectively. Mijia Dual-drum Dual Washer Dryer 10kg Mijia Dual-drum Dual Washer Dryer 10kg is expertly designed to offer easier sort-ability of laundry at home. With a 10kg capacity and an additional small drum specifically for intimate garments, it offers separation of different garment types. The dual drum independent drying system allows simultaneous drying, setting a new trend in zoned washing and drying. Equipped with advanced features such as smart triple dispensing, steam sterilization, and dual essence quick wash, it guarantees no cross-contamination throughout the washing process. High-temperature steam technology eliminates bacteria, mites, and allergens, meeting modern needs for high cleanliness. Mijia Dual-drum Dual Washer Dryer 10kg will be available for 4,999 RMB. Mijia Advanced Washer Dryer 10kg Pro Mijia Advanced Washer Dryer 10kg Pro is designed for users seeking efficient cleaning and garment care. Its special fabric care effectively reduces the risk of color transfer and enhances the whiteness of light-colored fabrics. By combining mechanical, chemical, and electrochemical washing methods, this washing machine offers a comprehensive upgrade in cleaning power. The unique 525mm large drum diameter design and 3D cold water wash technology ensure deep cleaning. Additionally, the built-in detergent, fabric softener storage, and app-customizable dispensing features provide significant convenience for users. Mijia Advanced Washer Dryer 10kg Pro will be available for 2,999 RMB. ENDS About Xiaomi Corporation Xiaomi Corporation was founded in April 2010 and listed on the Main Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on July 9, 2018 ( 1810.HK ). Xiaomi is a consumer electronics and smart manufacturing company with smartphones and smart hardware connected by an IoT platform at its core. Embracing our vision of “Make friends with users and be the coolest company in the users’ hearts”, Xiaomi continuously pursues innovations, high-quality user experience and operational efficiency. The company relentlessly builds amazing products with honest prices to let everyone in the world enjoy a better life through innovative technology. Xiaomi is one of the world’s leading smartphone companies, with its global smartphone shipments ranked among the top three and global MAU (including smartphones and tablets) reached 641.2 million in December 2023. The Company has also established the world’s leading consumer AIoT (AI+IoT) platform, reached approximately 739.7 million smart devices connected to its platform (excluding smartphones, laptops and tablets) as of December 31, 2023. In October 2023, Xiaomi upgraded its strategy to “Human × Car × Home” smart ecosystem, seamlessly merges personal devices, smart home products, and cars. Xiaomi always centers on humanity and is committed to providing comprehensive, better connected experiences. Xiaomi products are present in more than 100 countries and regions around the world. In August 2023, Xiaomi was included in the Fortune Global 500 list for the fifth year in a row, ranking 360th. Xiaomi is a constituent of the Hang Seng Index, Hang Seng China Enterprises Index, Hang Seng TECH Index and Hang Seng China 50 Index.
