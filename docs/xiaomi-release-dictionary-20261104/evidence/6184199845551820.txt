In addition to these options, classic options including 20\" Plum Blossom Rims, 20\" Mi‑shaped Rims, and 21\" Sport Rims are also available. Continuing this premium look, the new-generation Xiaomi SU7 series brings an electric front trunk to the entire lineup. In terms of materials, almost 100% of all high-frequency contact surfaces have also been wrapped in soft-touch finishes, while the steering is finished in Nappa leather and accented with chrome trim for enhanced luxury. Jewel‑like high‑gloss electroplated buttons electroplated buttons further elevate both tactile feedback and visual sophistication, and luxury quilting is applied to the door panels and seats, complemented by metal nameplates and metal speaker grilles for more exquisite expressions of craftsmanship. New Xiaomi Smart Chassis 2.0: Comprehensive upgrades across core electric systems for precise, composed driving and a up to 902 km max range The V6s Plus motor comes to the entire Xiaomi SU7 lineup alongside an upgraded high-voltage platform, delivering faster acceleration, lower energy consumption, quicker charging, and a range of up to 902 km (CLTC) The new-generation Xiaomi SU7 pushes performance further, with comprehensive upgrades across the entire powertrain. At the heart of this blistering performance, the entire lineup is equipped with the Xiaomi HyperEngine V6s Plus . With a maximum rotational speed of 22,000 rpm , it powers the car from 0–100 km/h in as little as 3.08 seconds and to a top speed of 265 km/h , marking a major breakthrough in high-performance electric drive. Range is also class-leading thanks to CTB (Cell-to-Body) integrated battery technology across the series with a pack volumetric energy density of up to 83.9% that boosts energy capacity without increasing volume. This enables a CLTC range of 720 km on the Standard model, 902 km on the Pro, and 835 km on the Max. Further improvements to energy efficiency come courtesy of upgraded energy flow management that increases the battery-to-wheel energy transfer efficiency to 94% , with combined consumption as low as 11.7 kWh/100 km for highly efficient energy utilization. Ultra-fast charging has also been upgraded, delivering up to 670 km of CLTC range in 15 minutes and charging from 10–80% SoC as quick as 12 minutes with a peak charging rate of 5.2C. New Xiaomi Smart Chassis 2.0 achieves leap in hardware, software, and intelligence to reinforce driving dynamics and safety The new-generation Xiaomi SU7 continues Xiaomi's commitment to setting the benchmark in its class , introducing an all-new Xiaomi Smart Chassis 2.0 with breakthroughs across hardware, software, intelligence, and real-world driving experience. Offering one of the best configurations in the RMB 300,000 segment, the Xiaomi Smart Chassis 2.0 features a double wishbone front and five-link independent rear suspension across all models. The chassis automatically detects road conditions and actively adjusts the closed dual-chamber air spring system and continuous damper control (CDC®) to absorb impacts and increase damping to stabilize the body for notable improvements in ride comfort. The system also supports variable suspension adjustment with location memory that automatically raises and lowers suspension based on GPS location for improved rides in variable terrain. Xiaomi Smart Chassis 2.0 also offers three suspension modes — Comfort, Sport, and Sport+ — with independently adjustable stiffness for instant response to road conditions. Multi-mode tuning expands the chassis bandwidth, delivering both dynamic performance and daily comfort. Suspension height is also adjustable across four levels from -20 mm to +30 mm (50 mm total) , allowing for improved clearance on rough roads and better aerodynamics and reduced drag on flat surfaces. On the software side, the new-generation Xiaomi SU7 enhances steering performance through refined system tuning. Reduced steering friction allows for a smoother ride, while the 12.5:1 steering ratio — lower than the typical 15:1 in family cars — ensures a quicker, more direct response. At 11.4 m, the turning diameter also stands above the segment average of 12 m, enabling tighter U-turns and other maneuvers perfect for urban driving. Even multi-lane maneuvers can be completed in a single, fluid motion, with smooth and precise control throughout. The upgraded breaking system now includes four-piston fixed front calipers across the lineup, maintaining consistent performance through 40 consecutive 100–0 km/h stops without fade (on the Max version). Both Standard and Pro models have enlarged NAO brake pads with a 30% larger contact surface area , offering improved quiet and comfort, while the Max model uses low-metallic brake pads for higher heat resistance and durability. Brake feel on all three models are tuned to match the Xiaomi SU7 Ultra, delivering faster responses, stronger feedback, and improved pedal precision. Complemented by 265 mm wide rear tires across the range, the vehicle delivers significantly enhanced stability and grip. Bringing further enhancements to the driving experience, Xiaomi Smart Chassis 2.0's Wet Road AI Detection technology has been updated to include an automatically-enabled enhanced stability control mode. Using multi-source data from rain sensors, external temperature sensors, microphones, and cameras, the system analyzes the environment in real-time to accurately judge road grip. Upon detecting wet surfaces, the system prompts the driver to switch to Wet Mode via a quick-access shortcut, which then automatically manages AWD torque distribution (on the Max version) and limits regenerative braking intensity. This process operates seamlessly without intrusive pop-ups, ensuring a smoother and safer experience. Moreover, Xiaomi's proprietary collaborative anti-slip control system reduces wheel slip up to 40% , with a fourfold increase in reaction speed for reactions in just 0.35 seconds . To reduce risks associated with vehicle sharing or valet use, Xiaomi SU7 also introduces an Owner Management Mode accessible via the central display or Xiaomi Auto app. Through the new mode, owners can perform functions like limiting power output across all drive modes while still enabling core functionalities, enabling one-tap toggle for high-performance features like Boost, Launch Control, specialized tuning to mitigate risks from accidental operation, and the ability to set flexible speed limits to ensure the vehicle remains in a safe and controllable state at all times. The system also incorporates the same Motion Sickness Relief Mode as Xiaomi YU7. Through coordinated optimization of power response and regenerative braking strategies, Motion Sickness Relief Mode significantly enhances acceleration and deceleration smoothness, helping to significantly reduce the likelihood of motion sickness and improve ride comfort and safety.
