Xiaomi Watch S5 46mm: Designed for Premium Style and Everyday Performance Xiaomi Watch S5 46mm combines advanced sports and health monitoring within a refined, lightweight design featuring a bold 46mm case, weighing just 46g, with an ultra-slim 10.99mm profile.¹ A premium stainless steel middle frame enhances durability and aesthetics, while optional stainless steel, ceramic, and forged carbon bezels offer added distinction. With a range of strap options, including new genuine leather materials, the watch adapts seamlessly to business, sports, and everyday settings. The display is further elevated by a 1.48-inch AMOLED display, featuring a 40% narrower bezel and up to 2,500 nits peak brightness,² ensuring clear visibility of notifications and health data even under bright sunlight. Powered by an 815mAh Xiaomi Surge Battery, delivering up to 21 days of battery life under light use,³ supporting continuous health monitoring and sports tracking with reduced charging frequency.⁴ To deliver more comprehensive everyday wellness insights, Xiaomi Watch S5 46mm features enhanced hardware and software capabilities. It introduces Passion Mode, which detects cheering gestures and converts them into measurable data, including heart rate, calories burned, and cheer counts. With over 150 sports modes, the device also integrates an upgraded 4 LED + 4 PD heart rate sensor and refined algorithms, improving accuracy to 98.4% for more reliable monitoring.⁵ An upgraded dual-band, five-system GNSS further ensures precise and stable tracking for outdoor activities. For sleep, new weekly and monthly reports, alongside sleep HRV tracking, provide deeper insights into long-term rest patterns.⁶ Developed in collaboration with leading international organizations, including the World Sleep Society, the Asian Society of Sleep Medicine, and the Chinese Sleep Research Society, these features deliver more science-based insights to support improved sleep health. Powered by Xiaomi HyperOS 3, Xiaomi Watch S5 46mm delivers a seamless connected experience with smartphones. Key functions — including remote photo capture, find my phone, earphone controls, and automatic synchronization of sports and health data — are accessible with ease. The device also supports integration with Xiaomi Home, enabling enhanced connectivity between wearables and smart home environments. Xiaomi Smart Band 10 Pro: Lightweight Design with Advanced Everyday Wellness Positioned as an everyday smart band, Xiaomi Smart Band 10 Pro combines comprehensive wellness tracking with an elevated design and broad compatibility. It features a 1.74-inch AMOLED display⁷ with up to 2,000 nits peak brightness for clear visibility.⁸ Despite the larger screen, Xiaomi Smart Band 10 Pro maintains a slim 9.7mm profile across the lineup, while weighing just 21.6g (excluding the Ceramic edition)⁹ for comfortable all-day wear. Crafted with a new generation of high-strength aluminum, it delivers durability and scratch resistance in a lightweight form. A 350mAh battery and low-power chipset enable up to 21 days of battery life under light usage conditions.¹⁰ Beyond design, it enhances health and fitness tracking with an upgraded PPG module and self-developed algorithms, improving heart rate accuracy to 98.2%.¹¹ With over 150 sports modes, it introduces track mode for lap recording and enhanced cycling mode for more precise workout tracking.¹² Sleep monitoring is also upgraded with sleep trend reports and deeper metrics such as HRV and sleep efficiency, while sleep algorithm 2.0 improves sleep onset and stage accuracy.¹¹ Moreover, Xiaomi Smart Band 10 Pro collaborates with Clue,¹³ a world-leading period and cycle tracking app, to deliver a more comprehensive approach to women's health management. With the purchase of Xiaomi Smart Band 10 Pro, users receive a 3-month free trial of Clue Plus. This premium subscription offers features like advanced cycle analysis, accurate fertility predictions, pregnancy and perimenopause tracking, and more. Users can bring together health and wellness data generated by their band with deeper menstrual health insights from Clue. The collaboration empowers people to better understand their well-being and make more informed health decisions. When connected to a Xiaomi smartphone, Xiaomi Smart Band 10 Pro supports notification synchronization and allows simultaneous pairing with an iPhone, enabling calls, messages, and alerts from both devices to be received on a single band.¹⁴ An optimized vibration motor differentiates alerts by message type, enhancing usability, while the NFC version¹⁵ enables secure contactless payments.¹⁶ The NFC version is available in Midnight Black, Glacier Silver, and a new Ceramic Edition, while the standard version comes in Midnight Black, Glacier Silver, and Lavender Pink, offering versatile style options. Xiaomi Buds 6: Refined Comfort with Immersive Audio As flagship semi-in-ear earbuds, Xiaomi Buds 6 feature new audio performance through the integration of enhanced hardware and software. It features an 11mm triple-magnet dynamic driver for impactful bass, paired with a 24k gold-plated diaphragm that improves treble sensitivity by 30% for crisper, clearer high notes.¹⁷ This is further refined by a suite of digital enhancements, including master-tuning by the Harman Golden Ear Team that introduces Harman Master Mode alongside Harman AudioEFX for superior sound quality, while Qualcomm® aptX™ Lossless technology enables high-resolution, lossless audio.¹⁸ Additionally, the system utilizes adaptive sound to maintain consistent mid-low frequencies across different ear shapes, while independent dimensional audio¹⁹ with head-tracking creates a fully immersive 360° soundstage. To ensure clear calls in a daily environment, the 3-mic AI noise reduction system, paired with a mesh-shaped acoustic structure, suppresses up to 95dB²⁰ of background noise and maintains voice clarity even in wind speeds of up to 12m/s.²¹ Combined with ANC featuring Balanced and Deep dual modes, users can choose the level of noise cancellation best suited to their surroundings for a more immersive listening experience. Engineered for comfort and seamless connectivity, Xiaomi Buds 6 deliver a lightweight fit and an intelligent cross-device experience. Each earbud weighs just 4.4g,²² featuring an ergonomic semi-in-ear design that follows the natural curve of the ear, reducing pressure and discomfort associated with traditional in-ear styles. The charging case is slim and portable, available in four colors: Graphite Black, Pearl White, Titan Gray, and Nebula Purple. On connectivity, Xiaomi Buds 6 integrates with Xiaomi HyperOS for effortless pairing and smooth switching across phones, tablets, and other Xiaomi devices. For the first time, Xiaomi earbuds debut compatibility with Apple Find My (Apple devices) and Google's Find Hub (Android™ devices).²³ This enables users to locate lost devices using the Apple or Find Hub networks.²⁴ Xiaomi Sound Play: Portable Sound with Dynamic Atmosphere Expanding on the Red Dot Award-winning Sound design language, Xiaomi Sound Play blends immersive audio with dynamic lighting in a compact, textile-wrapped design that seamlessly harmonizes sound, space, and atmosphere. Its 18W high-power sound engine produces room-filling sound, making it suitable for a variety of daily scenarios, from morning routines and workouts to outdoor gatherings and evening relaxation. Designed to elevate both sound and atmosphere, Xiaomi Sound Play features an infinity mirror light at the top and surrounding ambient lighting at the bottom. It offers five color effects and three lighting modes — Drumbeat, Water ripple, and Always-on — allowing users to tailor the visual experience to different environments. For connectivity, True Wireless Stereo (TWS) enables two units to function as separate left and right channels for a true stereo soundstage,²⁵ while party mode with Auracast™ supports up to 100 compatible speakers playing in sync,²⁶ delivering a coordinated audio and lighting experience for larger spaces or events. Built for quality and durability, Xiaomi Sound Play is engineered with IP68 dust and water resistance,²⁷ offering reliable protection against outdoor elements such as sand, rain, and dust. A 2,600mAh battery provides up to 14 hours of playback on a single charge,²⁸ with a full recharge in 2.5 hours. Available in Black, White, Green, and Purple, Xiaomi Sound Play offers a palette of diverse colorways to suit any lifestyle, merging portability, expressive design, and rugged performance into one versatile package.
