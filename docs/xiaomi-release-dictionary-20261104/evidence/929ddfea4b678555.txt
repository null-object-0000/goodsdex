[Beijing, China, September 25, 2025] - Xiaomi today held the 2025 Lei Jun Annual Speech and officially unveiled the new Xiaomi 17 series, along with Xiaomi Pad 8 series and a wave of new home appliances, at its annual flagship launch event held at the Beijing National Convention Center. In his sixth annual keynote titled "Change", Lei Jun highlighted Xiaomi's endeavors and resolution to advancing its "Human × Car × Home" smart ecosystem. The new Xiaomi 17 series comprises three models — Xiaomi 17, Xiaomi 17 Pro, and Xiaomi 17 Pro Max. A compact 6.3-inch flagship, Xiaomi 17 debuts the Snapdragon® 8 Elite Gen 5 Mobile Platform, a 7000mAh Xiaomi Surge Battery, and a Leica Summilux imaging system. Additionally, The 6.3-inch Xiaomi 17 Pro and the 6.9-inch Xiaomi 17 Pro Max also feature a revolutionary Dynamic Back Display, next-generation LOFIC High Dynamic Technology, and a 5x optical zoom lens. Xiaomi 17: Five Major Upgrades for Comprehensive Advance Compact Craftsmanship, All-Round Quality Upgrade Xiaomi 17 combines a refined 6.3-inch compact design with flagship capabilities. It features a sleek body at just 191g weight and 8.06mm thick, offering an ergonomic grip. The specially-designed large rounded corner features a super-elliptical design for a smoother, more refined feel. The subtly adjusted 19.6:9 screen ratio, complemented by a minimalist hole-free top design, offers a harmonious visual and handling experience. The new lightweight camera design along with matte glass also creates a light, smooth feel of the back. Innovative M10 Display Panel and SuperRED Luminous Material Xiaomi 17 further narrows bezels to just 1.18mm by adopting LIPO display packaging technology. It boasts a peak brightness of 3500 nits, ensuring a clear and readable viewing experience even in bright outdoor environments. Xiaomi 17 also incorporates innovative M10 Display Panel and SuperRED luminous material, a technology breakthrough leading in more advanced luminous efficiency. Leica Summilux System for Excellent Imaging Strength Featuring the Leica Summilux imaging system, Xiaomi 17 main camera uses a 50MP Light Fusion 950 sensor with ƒ/1.67 aperture and OIS, and the telephoto lens adopts a Leica 60mm floating lens design with ƒ/2.0 aperture and 10cm close-focus capability, delivering clear and vivid images across various harsh lighting conditions. Moreover, the new Master Portrait Mode offers professional-grade imaging with natural skin tones and detailed hair rendering. 7000mAh Xiaomi Surge Battery, Large Capacity in Compact Size Xiaomi 17 features a massive 7000mAh battery, the largest capacity among current 6.3-inch flagship phone lineup, breaking the trade-off between compact size and large capacity. Stemed from major innovations in the latest-generation Xiaomi Surge Battery, the battery achieves an ultra-high energy density of 894Wh/L, along with extended battery lifespan and excellent low-temperature endurance. The charging experience has also significantly upgraded, supporting 100W wired fast charging, 50W wireless charging, and compatibility with the 100W PPS universal charging protocol. Comprehensive Flagship Experience, Industry-Leading Communication Capabilities Xiaomi 17's communication capability reaches an industry-leading performance with global network coverage in over 210 countries and regions, ensuring stable and smooth network experience across various environment. The USB 3.2 Gen 1 high-speed port facilitates easy transfer of large files, significantly improving work efficiency. Xiaomi 17 Pro Series: Redefining the Next Generation of Premium Smartphones Xiaomi 17 Pro series, comprising Xiaomi 17 Pro and Xiaomi 17 Pro Max, embody Xiaomi's most revolutionary innovations, featuring a seamless symphony of dual displays and intelligent connection, powered by Snapdragon® 8 Elite Gen 5 Mobile Platform and an industry-leading powerhouse endurance. Xiaomi 17 Pro series enjoy a sleek 8mm-thick body design, and available in four colors — Black, White, Purple, and Green. Groundbreaking Dual-Screen Design Revolutionizes Mobile Interaction Xiaomi 17 Pro and Xiaomi 17 Pro Max introduce a revolutionary dual-screen design that blends sophisticated aesthetics with intuitive, intelligent functionality. The Dynamic Back Display enhances lifestyle and efficiency under various scenarios: customizable watch faces, AI wallpapers, virtual pets, dynamic information notifications, sticky notes, and so much more. In particular, users can enjoy the fun of creativity through various customized approaches — photos of pets or people can be processed into AI wallpapers and displayed on the back screen, supporting even dynamic wallpaper and with fun choices of portrait style. Moreover, the upgraded virtual pet feature introduces the third-generation pet — "Pangda" the panda, which could perform rich actions based on different scenarios and can also interact with user gestures. Meanwhile, the Dynamic Back Display provides a wide range of real-time notification, such as flight notifications, train ticket updates, takeaway delivery or ride-hailing status, music playback... the list goes on. The flexible pin feature allows users to pin schedules, QR codes, and even emojis on the secondary screen. Moreover, the back display supports both preview and selfie functions, allowing users to shoot selfies directly using the main cameras. It is also enhanced with advanced beauty algorithms and wide choices of filter for clearer, more natural-looking shots, along with unlimited imagination to shoot with the dual display. Close the main displays, this device will instantly transform into Handheld-Console Mode. It fits comfortably in one hand without missing any notifications shown on the back, and with the exclusive case connected via Bluetooth, it turns into a retro gaming console. Innovative Breakthrough in OLED Screen Technology Xiaomi 17 Pro series features the innovative M10 Display Panel and SuperRED luminous material, achieving excellent efficiency with ultra-high brightness and ultra-low power consumption. Additionally, Xiaomi 17 Pro Max exclusively adopts the independent pixel array technology, achieving sharper clarity while consuming less power than conventional 2K displays. Xiaomi 17 Pro Max is also equipped with Xiaomi Shield Glass 3.0, a super solid display glass to secure safe, sturdy use of the screen. Leica Summilux and LOFIC Technology to Achieve Pro-level Cinematic Imaging Xiaomi 17 Pro series features the Leica Summilux imaging system, meeting full creative camera needs. The main camera meets all-round optical capability upgrades, with an f/1.67 large aperture, a 1G+6P lens, and anti-glare lens coating, powered by Light Fusion 950L with a large 1/1.28-inch size sensor and next-generation LOFIC High Dynamic technology, achieving an ultra-high 16.5EV sensor dynamic range. Xiaomi 17 Pro debuts an inverted floating telephoto lens and supports 20cm telephoto macro shots. Xiaomi 17 Pro Max features a powerful 5x periscope telephoto lens with an expanded 1/2-inch sensor, an f/2.6 large aperture, and hardware-level HDR support. Its new light-gathering prism structure significantly enhances light capture, resulting in a 30% increase in light intake. Slim Design and Long Battery Life Showcasing Battery Tech Prowess Incorporating Xiaomi Surge Battery, Xiaomi 17 Pro series features a high 16% silicon content for high energy density, and L-shaped stacked battery maximizing internal space utilization to significantly boost energy density while maintaining a slim form factor. The breakthrough results in a 6300mAh battery for Xiaomi 17 Pro, while Xiaomi 17 Pro Max reaches 7500mAh. Xiaomi 17 Pro series models support 100W wired charging, 50W wireless charging, and the 100W PPS universal fast charging protocol, enabling convenient charging experience. Furthermore, with UWB-enabled smart car key functionality for keyless access and barrier-free interactions, the phone series redefines intelligent and integrated digital living with a previously unseen level of smart connectivity.
