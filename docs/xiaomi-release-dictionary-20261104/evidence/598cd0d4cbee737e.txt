With TÜV Rheinland Low Blue Light (Software Solution), Flicker-Free, and Circadian-Friendly certifications, the display is tuned for smooth visuals and comfortable viewing throughout the day. AI-powered camera and immersive audio for a richer experience Designed for everyday creativity and entertainment, REDMI 15 Series combines an intuitive camera with immersive sound and a more connected experience. Audio complements the visuals with Hi-Res Audio and Dolby Atmos®, plus a 200% volume boost for louder, clearer playback in busy environments.¹³ Built on Xiaomi HyperOS 2,¹ the series supports Xiaomi Interconnectivity—including call sync, network sync, and shared clipboard—for smoother multi-device workflows.¹⁴ It also works with Circle to Search with Google¹⁵ and Google Gemini¹⁶, streamlining daily workflow and productivity.
