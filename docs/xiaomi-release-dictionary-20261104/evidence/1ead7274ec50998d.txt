Available in three vibrant colors — Glacier White and Space Black — Redmi Buds 6 Pro are also available in Lavender Purple with excimer coating that ensures skin-friendly usage over long periods. Users can enjoy up to 9.5² hours of playback on a single charge or up to 36 hours² with the charging case, alongside intuitive touch controls for managing calls, audio playback, volume, and ANC modes. Its built-in linear motor provides quiet, precise haptic feedback, while Bluetooth® phone call support and dual-mic noise reduction provide clear calls in noisy or windy outdoor environments⁹. Redmi Watch 5 features a new in-house designed heart rate algorithm that improves heart rate monitoring accuracy to give users all-day tracking for heart rate¹⁰, blood oxygen, and sleep quality¹¹. Xiaomi 33W Power Bank 10000: Compact, Versatile Charging on the Go The perfect charging companion for people on the move, Xiaomi 33W Power Bank 10000 delivers fast and convenient charging in a compact form that easily slips into your bag or pocket. Its built-in USB-C cable gives users easy access to a 10,000mAh¹³ capacity power cell capable of charging a compatible Xiaomi smartphone to 45% in just 30 minutes¹⁴. Hitting a sweet spot in size and power, Xiaomi 33W Power Bank 10000 weighs 210 grams, making it lightweight enough to hold with one hand, eliminating hand strain or discomfort during charging. Its additional USB-C port offers 33W¹⁵ charging with an additional USB-A port supporting dynamic power allocation of up to 22.5W¹⁶ per device, with triple device charging bolstered with the ability to simultaneously self-charge at 30W. Xiaomi 165W Power Bank 10000: Ultra-Fast Full Power Charging For users seeking the absolute pinnacle of portable device charging, Xiaomi developed Xiaomi 165W Power Bank 10000. Convenient, fast, and versatile, it features dual ports capable of delivering a combined output of up to 165W¹⁷. Suited for charging smartphones, tablets, and laptop PCs, it can simultaneously charge two devices at charging speeds of up to 120W¹⁷ and 45W¹⁸. Tech-savvy Xiaomi device users can look forward to using 120W¹⁷ charging to achieve a full charge in as little as 23 minutes¹⁹. Housed in a sleek design and weighing only 318 grams, Xiaomi 165W Power Bank 10000 also features a built-in USB-C charging cable, making it ideal for users on the road. It features a smart display with colored icons that convey real-time power levels at a glance. Adaptable and versatile, Xiaomi 165W Power Bank 10000 supports the latest charging protocols. It offers dual port charging of up to 110W¹⁸ (65W + 45W) for non-Xiaomi devices, plus 90W self-charging²⁰ to achieve a 75% charge in just 15 minutes and a full charge in only 35 minutes²¹.
