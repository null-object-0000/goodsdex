Stickers take iPhone by storm
Thousands of iMessage Apps Offering Stickers Now Available on the App Store
Discover, download and launch new stickers and apps from the App Store for iMessage with iOS 10.
Just days after the release of iOS 10, iPhone and iPad users are already livening up their conversations with new, animated stickers from thousands of apps on the App Store — all available right from within Messages. Discover, download, and launch new stickers by tapping on the App Store icon right from within Messages. Simply tap to send a sticker or tap and hold to peel and place a sticker directly on top of a message bubble, photo or another sticker. You can even touch and hold on the sticker to adjust its size.
Available for the first time with iOS 10
, here’s a selection of some of the first sticker packs launching in the App Store for iMessage.
Colorful character stickers from doodle artist Jon Burgerman. Available in the App Store for iMessage with iOS 10.
Whimsical drawings by Marc Johns for Stickapax. Available in the App Store for iMessage with iOS 10.
Chill Hipster stickers to up your texting game. Available in the App Store for iMessage with iOS 10.
Live out your comic book fantasy with Comic Talk stickers. Available in the App Store for iMessage with iOS 10.
Stamps sticker pack to commemorate landmarks from cities around the world. Available in the App Store for iMessage with iOS 10.
Sago Mini stickers let Moms and Dads express themselves during the precious moments and chaotic times. Available in the App Store for iMessage with iOS 10.
