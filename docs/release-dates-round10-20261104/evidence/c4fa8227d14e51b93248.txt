iPhone 11 Pro, iPhone 11 Pro Max, iPhone 11 and Apple Watch Series 5 arrive in stores
The Redesigned Apple Fifth Avenue in New York Joins Apple Marunouchi and Apple Champs-Élysées in Welcoming First iPhone 11 Customers
iPhone 11 Pro, iPhone 11 Pro Max, iPhone 11 and Apple Watch Series 5 went on sale at 8 a.m. local time on Friday, September 20 around the world.
In New York, the new Apple Fifth Avenue has been completely reimagined from its landmark glass cube to a brighter and more expansive interior. Now nearly double the size, Apple Fifth Avenue reopened its doors today, coinciding with the global launch of iPhone 11, iPhone 11 Pro, iPhone 11 Pro Max and Apple Watch Series 5.
